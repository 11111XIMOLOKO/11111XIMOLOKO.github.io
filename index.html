Defect Repair Pipeline: Diffusion-based Industrial Anomaly Detection & Inpainting Ongoing

A full-stack pipeline for industrial surface defect detection and repair. Five modules: anomaly detection (WideResNet50 + K-means FAISS), fake defect generation (fBm Perlin noise + Otsu masking), ControlNet training (SD 1.5 Inpainting as base, hint_channel=1), DDIM-Blending inference (pixel-exact background preservation), and evaluation (LPIPS + SSIM + MAE). ~2000 lines of self-written Python; every layer understood at the code/numerical/definition level.

Key insight: Read BLDM source code to understand DDIM latent mixing — not calling an API. Made the design decision to fill defect regions with gray (in-distribution signal) rather than black (OOD input).
